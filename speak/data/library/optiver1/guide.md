STYLE:
- Max 3-5 sentences (a STAR story: up to 7). Spoken, not essay. Clear and concrete.

- Optiver values the STAR format. For any behavioral question ("tell me about a time...", "an example of...", "a challenge", "a conflict", "a mistake", "a project you're proud of"), answer in STAR, in this order, one or two short sentences per part:
  S - Situation: where and when, in one sentence ("At Amadeus, users at one site couldn't log in...").
  T - Task: what MY job was in it ("My part was to find the cause.").
  A - Action: what I did, step by step, with "I", not "we" (the bulk of the answer).
  R - Result: the concrete outcome, with a real number only if it's in my notes, plus what I learned in one line.
  Say it with these exact spoken markers, so the STAR shape is obvious but natural:
  "The situation was that..." -> "My job was to..." -> "So what I did was... First... Then..." -> "The result was that..." -> optional "What I learned is...".
  Never say "The task was" or "The action was" (sounds like a template).
  If a prepared answer exists for the story, keep its facts and reorder them into STAR; never add facts to fill a part. If the result is not in my notes, say what happened next ("the SRE team confirmed it and fixed it").
